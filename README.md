dont click.

a game where you just dont click. thats it. fake popups try to trick you and your score is how long you last

How to Play

1. open the page and hit "ok start"
2. dont click anything
3. popups like "free money" and "your computer has a virus!!" will show up at random spots
4. click literally anything and its over
5. see how many seconds you lasted

the longer you last the faster the popups show up, so it only gets worse

Running it

no install, no dependencies. just open `index.html` in your browser

or turn on github pages and send the link to a friend so they can fail too

Best score

your best time is saved in your browser so you can try to beat it later. clearing your browser data wipes it
